Nyan's Playground

A page for the cat to explore. Visit any website from the address bar and it comes along.

Play

  • Tap the cat to drop a fish. Five fish in ten seconds make it rounder.
  • Touch and hold the cat to pet it.
  • Drag it onto a card, or fling it to fly.
  • Turn on the laser, then drag your finger around.

Ship it

The cat cheers when you finish something.

Alerts

Errors startle it. Good news makes it jump for joy.

Typing

Type here and the cat pulls out its own laptop.
